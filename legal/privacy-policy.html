This Privacy Policy describes how information may be handled when you visit this website, submit an inquiry, adjust cookie preferences, or otherwise interact with the online services provided under the CDLWAY brand.

This policy is intended to provide general transparency about website data practices. Additional notices may be provided when a particular form, service, legal requirement, or processing activity requires more specific information.

01

Scope and Application

This Privacy Policy applies to information collected through this website and the forms, preference tools, and interactive features made available through it.

It does not automatically apply to independent driving schools, employers, testing authorities, financial assistance providers, sponsors, advertisers, external websites, or other organizations that may be mentioned or linked from this website.

Those organizations may operate under their own privacy policies, legal responsibilities, data systems, and retention practices. You should review their notices before providing information directly to them.

Important Scope Notice A general website inquiry does not create enrollment, employment, financial aid approval, sponsorship, licensing, testing, or another formal relationship.
02

Information You Provide

Information may be collected when you complete a website form, send an email, request program details, ask about a schedule, submit a financial assistance question, request career information, inquire about sponsorship, or propose a business collaboration.

Program and training inquiries

Depending on the form, information may include:

  • Your full name.
  • Your email address.
  • The CDL program or service that interests you.
  • Your current license or permit status.
  • Your preferred training format or approximate start period.
  • Your career direction or employer-related preferences.
  • The questions, goals, circumstances, or other information included in your message.

Financial assistance inquiries

You may choose to identify the program you are considering, the type of assistance information you need, and general questions about tuition, payment planning, eligibility, sponsorship, deadlines, or documentation.

Do not use a general inquiry form to send financial account details, tax documents, government identification numbers, medical records, payment-card information, passwords, or similar sensitive records.

Business and collaboration inquiries

Information may include your name, email address, organization, collaboration type, proposed scope, objectives, timing, and any budget context you voluntarily provide.

Information contained in correspondence

If you communicate by email, information contained in the message and any permitted attachments may be processed to review and respond to the communication.

03

Automatic Information Collection

Website infrastructure may automatically process technical information when a browser or device connects to the website. The exact information depends on the hosting, security, analytics, consent, and performance tools enabled at the time.

Technical information may include:

  • Internet Protocol address or an abbreviated version of it.
  • Browser type, language, and version.
  • Device type, operating system, and screen characteristics.
  • Date, time, duration, and approximate interaction sequence.
  • Referring page, requested page, and navigation path.
  • Error, performance, security, and diagnostic information.
  • General geographic information inferred from technical connection data.
  • Cookie, storage, or consent identifiers where permitted and enabled.

Technical logs may be necessary to deliver the website, identify errors, maintain availability, investigate abuse, prevent fraudulent submissions, and protect system security.

Analytics Configuration Optional analytics or advertising technologies should be activated only in accordance with the selected consent settings and applicable legal requirements.

  • Responding through the email address you provide.

  • Asking follow-up questions when additional context is reasonably necessary.

Security and compliance

  • Preventing spam, abuse, fraud, or malicious activity.
  • Investigating security events and protecting website systems.
  • Maintaining records where reasonably necessary for legal, regulatory, accounting, dispute, or compliance purposes.
  • Enforcing applicable website terms and protecting legal rights.

Communications

The website is designed primarily for request-based communication. Marketing communications should be sent only where an appropriate legal basis and any required consent exist.

When Information May Be Shared

Information is not intended to be sold as part of the normal operation of this inquiry website. Information may, however, be disclosed in the limited circumstances described below.

Website service providers

Vendors may process information when providing hosting, infrastructure, form delivery, email, security, analytics, consent management, accessibility, technical support, or related operational services.

Service providers should receive only the access reasonably necessary to perform their functions and should be subject to appropriate contractual, confidentiality, security, and data protection obligations.

Professional advisers

Information may be shared with legal, accounting, insurance, security, compliance, or other professional advisers when reasonably necessary for legitimate business or legal purposes.

Legal requirements and protection

Information may be disclosed when reasonably believed to be necessary to:

  • Comply with applicable law or a valid legal process.
  • Respond to a lawful government or regulatory request.
  • Protect the rights, safety, property, or security of others.
  • Investigate fraud, abuse, malicious activity, or security events.
  • Establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.

Business changes

Information may be transferred as part of a merger, acquisition, financing, reorganization, sale of assets, insolvency process, or another business transaction, subject to applicable legal requirements.

At your direction

Information may be shared when you request or authorize the disclosure. Before directing information to an independent organization, review that organization’s privacy practices.

08

Data Retention

Information should be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, unless a longer period is required or permitted by law.

Retention decisions may consider:

  • The nature and status of the inquiry.
  • Whether follow-up communication remains reasonably expected.
  • The sensitivity and amount of information.
  • Security, fraud-prevention, and system-log requirements.
  • Applicable legal, regulatory, accounting, or reporting duties.
  • Limitation periods and the need to establish or defend claims.
  • Whether information can be aggregated or de-identified.

When information is no longer reasonably needed, it may be deleted, anonymized, aggregated, or isolated from routine use, subject to technical and legal limitations.

Backup Systems Deleted information may remain temporarily in secured backup systems until the relevant backup cycle is completed.
09

Data Security

Reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards should be used to protect website information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, loss, misuse, or destruction.

Safeguards may include:

  • Encrypted transport where supported.
  • Access controls and authentication.
  • Restricted administrative permissions.
  • Security logging and monitoring.
  • Software maintenance and vulnerability management.
  • Spam, abuse, and malicious-input controls.
  • Service provider review and contractual protections.
  • Backup, recovery, and incident-response procedures.

No website, transmission method, storage system, or security control can provide an absolute guarantee of protection. You should avoid sending unnecessary sensitive information and should use official secure channels when an organization requests confidential documents.

Security Reporting Suspected website security issues may be reported through the corporate email address listed in this policy.

Your Privacy Choices

Depending on your jurisdiction and the circumstances of the processing, you may have some or all of the rights described below.

  • Access: request information about personal data being processed.
  • Correction: request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Deletion: request deletion where applicable legal conditions are met.
  • Restriction: request limits on certain processing activities.
  • Objection: object to processing based on certain legitimate interests or to direct marketing.
  • Portability: request eligible information in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Consent withdrawal: withdraw consent for future processing where consent is the legal basis.
  • Complaint: contact an appropriate data protection authority where that right applies.

Submitting a request

A privacy request may be sent to the corporate email address listed below. The request should clearly identify the right you wish to exercise and provide enough information to locate the relevant interaction.

Reasonable steps may be required to verify identity and protect information from unauthorized disclosure. Additional information should be requested only when reasonably necessary for verification or clarification.

Limitations and exceptions

Privacy rights are not absolute. A request may be limited or denied where permitted by law, including when information is required for legal compliance, security, fraud prevention, another person’s rights, or the establishment or defense of legal claims.

  • Vendor assessments and security requirements.
  • Data minimization and access limitations.
  • Other legally recognized transfer safeguards.

Privacy and government-access laws may differ between jurisdictions. Questions about applicable transfer safeguards may be submitted through the corporate email address.
