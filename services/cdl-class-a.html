Core Class A Skills

Develop the Skills Behind Combination Vehicle Operation

Class A training should connect knowledge of the vehicle to controlled movement, observation, communication, and safer road decision-making.

01

Pre-Trip Inspection

Review tractor, trailer, brake, coupling, lighting, tire, fluid, control, and safety components.

02

Coupling Concepts

Understand connection checks, fifth-wheel components, air lines, electrical lines, and safe verification steps.

03

Vehicle Controls

Build familiarity with gauges, braking systems, transmission behavior, steering, mirrors, and cab controls.

04

Backing Fundamentals

Practice setup, reference points, controlled corrections, observation, and stopping when the position is uncertain.

05

Turns and Positioning

Develop lane positioning, trailer tracking awareness, clearance judgment, mirror use, and safe turn setup.

06

Road Observation

Strengthen scanning, following distance, speed management, hazard identification, and communication with other road users.

Real-World Class A Preparation

Train for the Full Vehicle Combination

Effective Class A preparation goes beyond steering the tractor. It requires an understanding of how the trailer changes space, movement, visibility, braking, and decision-making.

01

Vehicle Awareness

Understand overall length, width, trailer movement, blind areas, and clearance considerations.

02

Controlled Backing

Develop setup habits, slow corrections, mirror checks, and safe stopping decisions.

03

Road Positioning

Practice lane control, trailer tracking, turn space, and appropriate following distance.

04

Testing Readiness

Review inspection communication, control skills, road habits, and applicable testing expectations.

Class A Training Route

Follow the Route from Knowledge to Road Preparation

Each stage provides context for the next, helping students connect the vehicle, the yard, the road, and applicable testing expectations.

01

Knowledge Preparation

Commercial driving concepts, vehicle systems, safety, and applicable written-test topics

02

Pre-Trip Inspection

Tractor, trailer, coupling, brake, control, and safety checks

03

Yard Development

Controls, setup, straight-line backing, offset movement, and positioning

04

Road Practice

Turns, scanning, spacing, speed control, communication, and hazard response

05

Testing Preparation

Readiness review, practice feedback, and applicable next-step planning
