Applicants should compare the training provider, covered costs, license path, equipment, expected employment, compensation structure, route assignment, location, scheduling, and consequences of leaving the agreement early.

Company-sponsored training is not guaranteed. Availability, eligibility, training coverage, employment, compensation, routes, schedules, and agreement terms vary by employer and applicant.

Written Commitment Questions

Agreement Requirements

The agreement should clearly explain the employer’s training support and the applicant’s financial, training, work, location, schedule, and service obligations.

Covered Training Costs

Identify the exact tuition, fees, testing, licensing, travel, lodging, materials, or other costs the employer agrees to cover.

Minimum Service Period

Confirm when the commitment begins, how long it lasts, which employer or affiliate it covers, and how service time is calculated.

Repayment Conditions

Review when repayment applies, how the balance is calculated, whether it decreases over time, and which events trigger the obligation.

Assigned Location

Ask whether training, orientation, terminal assignment, relocation, regional placement, or travel may be required.

Employment Conditions

Confirm whether successful training guarantees employment, requires additional testing, or remains subject to employer approval and qualification.

Early-Exit Provisions

Review resignation, termination, failed testing, medical disqualification, relocation refusal, leave, and other possible exit scenarios.

After Training

Testing and Qualification

Orientation and Equipment

Ask about company procedures, safety policies, assigned equipment, freight, technology, documentation, and vehicle training.

Route and Schedule

Compare local, regional, longer-distance, overnight, weekend, rotating, or assigned-route expectations.

Performance Standards

Review attendance, safety, documentation, customer, productivity, communication, training, and continued qualification expectations.
