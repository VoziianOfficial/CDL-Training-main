Core Class B Skills

Develop Control for Straight Trucks and Applicable Buses

Training should help students connect the size and operating characteristics of a Class B vehicle to safer inspection, maneuvering, and road decisions.

01

Vehicle Inspection

Review the engine area, steering, suspension, brakes, tires, lights, cab controls, emergency equipment, and exterior condition.

02

Cab and Control Familiarity

Build confidence with gauges, braking, steering, mirrors, transmission behavior, warning systems, and vehicle-specific controls.

03

Backing Fundamentals

Practice setup, mirror use, reference points, straight movement, controlled corrections, and safe stopping.

04

Turns and Clearance

Develop turn setup, lane positioning, rear-wheel tracking, mirror checks, curb awareness, and clearance judgment.

05

Stopping and Speed Control

Understand braking distance, smooth stopping, speed selection, following distance, and changing road conditions.

06

Road Observation

Strengthen scanning, mirror routines, communication, hazard recognition, lane control, and awareness of vulnerable road users.

Real-World Class B Preparation

Train for the Vehicle, Route, and Responsibility

Class B preparation should address more than basic movement. Students need to understand vehicle size, visibility, stopping, route conditions, cargo or passenger responsibilities, and employer expectations.

01

Vehicle-Specific Awareness

Understand dimensions, blind areas, turning behavior, clearance, and the controls of the training vehicle.

02

Controlled Maneuvers

Practice slow-speed movement, backing, positioning, stopping, and corrections within a structured yard environment.

03

Route Awareness

Build observation habits for intersections, lane changes, pedestrian activity, loading areas, and local traffic.

04

Testing Readiness

Review inspection communication, control skills, road habits, and applicable Class B testing expectations.
